Does “not included for its own sake” mean “destroy it”?

No. An entity can still be protected because people value it, because protection has good effects, or because caution is useful. The answer only says that the entity’s own interests do not directly enter the decision under the stated facts.

The main lesson

You can explain who counts without pretending that everyone must receive equal weight.

Conscious experience, the ability to act, interests, relationships, group membership, objective status, and human-made rules can overlap or separate. A complete view says which features are enough for inclusion, how uncertainty is handled, and why its exclusions do not simply repeat familiar prejudice.
