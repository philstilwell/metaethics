Is a learned feeling fake?

No. Shame, guilt, anger, and felt demands are real experiences even when family, religion, law, or group life helped shape them. But a feeling’s reality does not prove that its judgment is accurate or that an objective moral fact caused it.

Moral Intuitions without a Moral Realm traces how learned emotions and rules can become fast, sincere, and hard to recognize as learned.

Is this a mental-health assessment?

No. It maps possible jobs for emotions. It does not diagnose distress or tell anyone to stop feeling on command. Severe or lasting distress may call for human or professional support beyond this site.

The main lesson

Accountability can survive without treating suffering as deserved for its own sake.

People can name harm, defend trust, protest, require repair, and express commitments. The remaining task is to shape emotion around those purposes without preserving prejudice, manipulation, permanent shame, or punishment for its own sake.
